## Retracted or corrected (kept on purpose)

1. **"The firmware says 2 GiB RAM."** Every `dtbo` entry carries
   `/memory@40000000` with size `0x80000000`. Wrong as a RAM reading: the board DT
   still says `compatible=fsl,imx8mm-evk`, and 2 GiB is the EVK default that U-Boot
   overwrites. The live unit's `MemTotal: 3979544 kB` is impossible on 2 GiB.
   RAM is 4 GB from the device, and the firmware does not settle it.
2. **"One NVP key opens both models."** It seemed plausible because the vendor
   trees are shared (the ZX507 vendor carries `DseeAi_ICX1293.*`). Refuted by
   the SHA-224 mismatch above.
3. **"Android 11/12 have no `p/` kernel configs."** My first loop over
   `kernel/configs` printed nothing for `p/` on android10/11/12. A second request
   returned HTTP 200 and `android-4.14 android-4.4 android-4.9` for 11, 12 and 13.
   The first loop had failed silently: jq on an empty body exits 0, so the
   `|| echo none` fallback never ran. The table in the feasibility doc uses the
   second reading.
4. **"`fastboot oem unlock` succeeds"** (2026-09-24 devlog, from community notes).
   Not contradicted, but not confirmed either: the bootloader strings show
   `flashing unlock`, and no `oem unlock` string. Left open for the device stage.

## Touch dead: Himax firmware upload loses a race with ueventd (22:38-23:03)

- User report after the first boot: the screen flickers badly and touch does
  nothing. `getevent` on event1 (`himax-touchscreen`) recorded no events
  (`v5-getevent-touch.txt`, `v5-getevent-touch2.txt`).
- The panel is a Himax HX83102D TDDI in zero-flash mode: the touch MCU runs
  from SRAM, and the driver uploads `Himax_firmware_30B.bin` on every boot.
  Probe (`himax_common.c`) calls `request_firmware_nowait`, which falls back to
  the user helper (ueventd), and queues `himax_mcu_0f_operation` 10 s later;
  that work gives up if the callback has not delivered the firmware yet. The
  kernel cmdline has `firmware_class.path=/vendor/firmware`, but /vendor is not
  mounted at probe time.
- `v5b-kmsg` (boot at 22:45): `Falling back to user helper` at 1.375 s,
  `[HXTP][ERROR] Failed TP firmware update` at 11.487 s, `ueventd started!` at
  12.686 s. The GSI compiles its SELinux policy in first stage init, because the
  vendor's precompiled policy does not match it, and the debug wrapper's wait
  adds about 2 s. Together they push ueventd past the 10 s work. Resume does
  not retry the upload (`HX_RESUME_HW_RESET` is not defined).
- Unbind/bind of `spi1.1` under `/sys/bus/spi/drivers/himax_tp` was a dead end.
  The rebound driver logged `private_ts->hx_irq is empty, use polling mode`,
  then went into an ESD loop: `ALL Zero event`, then `ESD - Reset`, then
  `himax_mcu_0f_operation_dirly`, about every 0.45 s (120 resets left in the
  ring, `v5-dmesg-rebind.txt`). The unit was rebooted at 22:45:04 to clear it.
- Runtime proof, 22:52:55: the late callback had still stored the firmware in
  `ts->fw_entry`, so re-queueing the 0f work through the driver's debug node
  (`echo p > /proc/android_touch/debug`) ran the stock update path:
  `Entering Update Flow!`, `CID_VER : 30B`, `TP firmware update END`
  (`v5b-debug-p.txt`). `getevent` 22:53:16-23:03:16 recorded 50
  `BTN_TOUCH DOWN` while the user used the screen
  (`v5b-getevent-after-p.txt`).
- Fix: `kernel/patches/0003-himax-wait-for-firmware-request.patch`. The async
  callback completes `fw_entry_done`, and the 0f work waits on it instead of
  giving up. A failed request still completes it, at the loader's own timeout.
  Made as a commit in the kernel tree (`446aa67` shipped sources, `7495180`
  patch). aws-burst build 6 started 22:49:58.

## No sound in the earphones: IzmAudioManager never chose the output (20:08-20:23)

Capture: `device-capture/20261015T1112Z-no-sound/`.

- 20:08 user: "이어폰 꽂았는데 이쪽으로 소리가 안 나오는 것 같음" (the
  earphones are in, but nothing seems to come out of them).
- The jack and routing were fine: `SW_HEADPHONE_INSERT*`, AudioService has
  `type:0x8 (headphone)` connected, and STREAM_MUSIC is on `headphone(8)`.
- The codec was not:
  - playing at 20:09:10, all three card-1 PCMs read `closed`;
  - the mixer showed `output device off` (`tinymix-idle.txt`,
    `tinymix-play.txt`).
  - The HAL had received `izm_output_device=none` at boot (`MixerCtrl:
    Set: output device, off`), and nothing sent another value after that.
- Cause, from the decompiled IzmAudioManager:
  - Its `HEADSET_PLUG` receiver calls `updateConnected()`, which reads
    `izm_jack_status_se/btl` from the HAL.
  - When the wired state changed, it calls
    `setWiredDeviceConnectionState(8, ...)` and returns false. It then waits
    for the HEADSET_PLUG that Android 9's AudioService sent back, and only
    that second broadcast runs `updateOutput()`.
  - On the GSI, AudioService has already connected the headphone from the
    input event (devinputjack). The call changes nothing, no second
